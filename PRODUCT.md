# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, recruiters, and engineering peers reviewing a software engineer's work. They need to understand technical focus, evidence of impact, and professional background quickly on desktop or mobile.

## Product Purpose

Present a concise, credible software engineering portfolio through selected projects, technical writing, a short biography, and a directly accessible Resume. Success means a visitor can understand the engineer's strengths and reach the most relevant evidence without navigating inherited scholarly or template content.

## Brand Personality

Precise, confident, understated. The interface should feel deliberate and technically literate without performing technicality as decoration.

## Anti-references

Avoid trendy developer clichés: terminal cosplay, neon gradients, skill meters, excessive animation, generic technology icon walls, glassmorphism, and template-like card grids.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with evidence. Projects, writing, and the Resume must be easier to reach than decorative content.
2. Keep the interface quiet. Hierarchy comes from typography, spacing, and clear labels.
3. Write directly. Copy describes specific work and actions without marketing language.
4. Make empty states intentional. Missing personal content must never look broken or fabricated.
5. Preserve user control. Documents and external work must be available through explicit, accessible links.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard requirements. Preserve visible focus states, semantic landmarks, useful link labels, reduced-motion behavior, and non-embedded access to the Resume PDF. Embedded content must never be the only way to reach information.
